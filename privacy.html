How Advanced Finance Tracker handles your data.

What We Collect

Advanced Finance Tracker only stores the data you enter directly into the app: transaction title, amount, category, date, your theme preference, and your storage-consent choice. We do not collect IP addresses, device identifiers, analytics events, or any information that you have not typed into the form yourself.

How We Use It

Your data is used solely to render summaries, charts, and the transaction list inside this single page. It powers nothing else. There is no advertising, profiling, or third-party data sharing because there is no server-side component to share with.

Storage Location

All data is stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage on this device. Although localStorage is not technically a cookie, the EU ePrivacy Directive and the UK ICO classify any non-essential client-side storage as a "tracking technology" requiring consent — that is why this app shows a consent banner on first visit. Your data never leaves your browser and is never transmitted to us, our hosting provider, or any third party.

Your Rights

  • Right to access: all of your data is already visible to you in the app and inside your browser's developer tools (Application → Local Storage).
  • Right to erasure: click "Reject" on the consent banner, clear your browser's site data for this domain, or delete individual transactions from the app — any of these fully removes your data.
  • Right to withdraw consent: clear your browser's site data to reset the consent banner and choose again on your next visit.
  • Right to portability: use the Export CSV button on the home page to download all of your transactions in a portable format.

Contact

This is a course project hosted as a static page. If you have any privacy-related questions, please contact the project owner via the repository's issue tracker.
